## Change
1. **Grayscale-first hierarchy pass on the airport sheet (F11).**
   - Fixed-width frequency label column with standard abbreviations (CTAF, UNICOM, ATIS, AWOS, ASOS, TWR, GND, CD, APP, DEP, CTR, FSS), so labels never hyphenate.
   - Value emphasis over labels. Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for all text (no faint "Asphalt").
   - Section order by J1 relevance: identity + category, then weather, frequencies, runways, NOTAMs, charts, PIREPs, winds.
   - The sheet opens at a useful height (current detents are .medium/.large).
2. **Spacing and type scale tokens** (4/8/12/16/24/32) applied across the sheet sections and layer panel. Record the tokens in
   docs/DESIGN.md `## Tokens` and `## Typography`, and the components in `## Components`.
3. **F17.** Split the layer panel into "Base map" (Street / Satellite / Terrain) and "Sectional" (toggle + opacity), instead of
   "VFR" being a base style. Keep AppState compatible: map the existing `mapStyle` + `visibleLayers.sectional`, and coordinate
   with MapService.setMapStyle (the sectional layer is `base-vfr-sectional`).
4. **F13.** The ownship chevron gets a contrasting halo/outline. Airport symbols get a contrasting outline. They must be
   legible over the yellow city tint of the sectional at 70% opacity.
5. **F10.** Make flight planning reachable: Flights opens on "Plans" when there is no flight history, and the map has a Plan
   entry point (a small route button near search, or similar).
