# IL-13: Israeli airport diagrams on zoom, like the US (dashboard ticket T11)

Owner request: when you zoom into an Israeli aerodrome, its official aerodrome diagram appears georeferenced on the map,
the way the FAA airport diagrams do in the US. In the US this is T11/T17:
- `efb-212/Services/AirportDiagramGeoreferencer.swift` and `AirportDiagramOverlay.swift`;
- `tools/apd-georef/`;
- the report docs/design/apd-georef-report.md;
- a 50 ft accuracy gate validated against runway ends;
- legibility day and night (T18).
Reuse all of it. Don't build a second diagram system.

Hosting follows the owner's chart decision (Q5): we convert, validate and host. The app downloads from our Cloudflare R2
host (WEB-1 is building `web/charts/`), never from gov.il. The diagrams are © CAAI; the owner is handling permission, so
build locally and don't publish.

## Part A: Inventory and pipeline
1. **Inventory** what exists and record it in docs/research/israel-cvfr.md (new subsection), with links:
   - the eAIP AD 2.24 aerodrome and parking charts for LLBG, LLHA and LLER (https://e-aip.azurefd.net/, current AIRAC);
   - the domestic AIP chapter ד aerodrome charts (Herzliya LLHZ, Rosh Pina LLIB, Ein Yahav LLEY, Bar Yehuda/Masada
     LLMZ, Kiryat Shmona LLKS, and others) and any chapter ה airstrip sketches.
   For each aerodrome note: whether a chart exists, its scale or "not to scale", whether it's vector, its language and
   edition. Sketches that aren't to scale can't be georeferenced; list them as "sheet only" (PDF viewer), like the US
   fallback.
   Download with the browser into `tools/caai-apd/input/` (gitignored). The eAIP host may allow curl; try it.
2. **Georeference:** extend `tools/apd-georef` (preferred) or add a thin `tools/caai-apd` wrapper that reuses its fitting
   and validation code.
   - Control truth: the CAAI GIS runway ends in `efb-212/Resources/israel-aviation.sqlite` (`runways`, 42 rows), plus
     the eAIP AD 2.12 threshold coordinates for LLBG, LLHA and LLER.
   - Keep the 50 ft gate (same reason as T17). A diagram that fails is shown as "sheet only", never misplaced.
   - Mask insets and tables, as T11 did for BOS.
3. **Output:** a diagrams pack (per-aerodrome image plus georeference JSON, the same format the US overlay consumes),
   with a manifest entry under the same R2 manifest scheme IL-9 uses (product `il-diagrams`, an edition, checksums,
   validation results, `published: false`). Write a coverage report, like apd-georef-report.md: N of M georeferenced and
   why each failure failed.
