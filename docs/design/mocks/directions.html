Kanaf design directions: mocks for Q1

Plain HTML, not the app. Each direction shows the same two screens with the same sample data: in flight near KPAO on the sectional, and the Israel CVFR debrief of a sample LLHZ to LLIB flight. The chart is a drawn stand-in, not FAA artwork. Look at type, color, surfaces and the one signature element per direction; sizes are scaled down from the iPad. In every direction red stays warning, amber stays caution, magenta stays the route.

A. Chart roomB. Glass cockpitC. Motion on ground screensD. Today (stay HIG)

A. Chart room (recommended)

The app speaks the language of the chart the pilot already reads: condensed capital labels, tick-mark rules, airspace blue for airport data blocks. The signature is the debrief strip map, a hand-drawn (Canvas) strip of the flight with nautical-mile ticks, reporting points in English and Hebrew, and the altitude flown against each leg's target band.

Changes: type (IBM Plex Sans Condensed + Hebrew for labels and headings), ground-screen layouts, one Canvas strip mapKeeps: SF for numbers, navy controls, in-flight layoutRisk: low; adds two font files (free licence)

B. Glass cockpit

The in-flight chrome becomes an avionics panel drawn with Canvas: a heading tape, a wind arrow, bezel-style keys, and the strip in avionics colors (white for current values, cyan for selected, magenta for the active leg). Ground screens follow in dark.

Changes: every in-flight surface, colors, controlsKeeps: map, tab structureRisk: high: redraws the safety-critical screen, dark panels by day are harder to read in sunlight, no CFI review yet

C. Motion on ground screens

Today's look and type stay. Ground screens gain purposeful motion: the debrief draws the flight path, the ownship marker flies it once, and the leg results arrive in order. The in-flight screen does not move at all, as today. Reduce Motion turns every animation off.

Changes: transitions on Flights, debrief, replay, onboardingKeeps: everything visualRisk: lowest; does not give the app a new identity

D. Today (stay HIG)

The current Kanaf look for reference: SF Pro, navy Liquid Glass controls, light solid panels with a navy hairline, grouped lists on ground screens. The new skills would only be used to check code.

Changes: nothingKeeps: everythingRisk: none; the app keeps looking like a well-made settings screen
