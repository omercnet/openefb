# M4 port notes (airport diagram overlay, PDF chart viewer)

## Decision Q6 applied

`AirportDiagramGeoreferencer.swift` (1604 lines, PDFKit + Vision) is NOT ported. Georeferencing and rendering happen offline
in `tools/apd-georef` and `tools/caai-apd` and ship in the chart packs; the app only places a rendered image by four
corners. Its 11 Swift tests (`AirportDiagramGeoreferencerTests`) stay with the Swift tool, and the pack-side loading and
validation belong to S1c (`services/charts/diagramPack.ts`, `diagramGeoreference.ts`).

## Reconciled with S1c (#51)

M4's earlier stand-ins are gone; it now uses S1c's types:

- Pack: `adapters/israelDiagramSource.ts` implements the overlay's `DiagramPackSource` over S1c's installed `IsraelDiagramPack`
  (`georeferencedICAOs`, `availability`) and `loadGeoreference` (decoded, validated, invertible-or-nothing). Corners are
  `clipCornersForImageSource` (TL, TR, BR, BL as [lon, lat]), passed through untouched as `DiagramOverlayImage.coordinates`;
  the backing polygon is built from the same quad.
- PDF: `adapters/terminalPdfSource.ts` implements `ChartPdfSource` over `TerminalProcedureServiceLike.pdfPath` and
  `cycleWithCode(...).expirationMs`; the viewer's chart type is S1c's `TerminalChart`.
- Swift `georeferencedAerodromes(pack:near:limit:)` offers the nearest few of the WHOLE pack (no radius); the adapter does the
  same (the camera radius is ignored for Israel), the controller sorts and takes the nearest three. The Swift
  `nearestGeoreferencedAerodromes` case is ported end to end (pack, adapter, controller).
- The US has no precomputed diagram pack: S1c ships only `il-diagrams`. US candidates are empty and `diagramFor` is
  unavailable, so no US diagram is drawn until a US pack exists (owner decision Q6 also covers the US; that needs a pack
  built by `tools/apd-georef` and a S1c product). This is a visible gap versus Swift, which georeferenced US diagrams on
  device. The US candidate filter and the Swift database query are therefore not ported.

## Swift behaviour preserved

Min zoom 14, preload from 13.5, at most 3 airports per refresh (nearest first, fixed-wing only), 0.85 raster opacity,
backing 0.85 to 0.97 across zoom 14 to 15 (white by day, 10% grey at night), night ink brightness 0.72 to 0.82, a 5 minute
retry after a failed load, "unavailable" sticking for the session, region change clearing everything, remote kill switch.

## JS-only changes

- R6: a fixed pool of 3 slots (backing fill + image source + raster layer each) instead of adding sources and layers per
  airport. With all slots used, the diagram farthest from the camera centre is evicted for a newer one (Swift kept every
  entry it ever loaded).
- Loads carry an epoch: a region change or dispose discards results still in flight (tested by removing the guard).
- Camera polling every 0.75 s is replaced by the surface's settled-camera callback; the 1e-5 degree skip is not needed.
- `raiseAboveIsraelFills` and the airspace tint reordering are not needed: layer order is the static registry order, with
  the diagram slots below the airport dots and above the sectional. In Israel the CVFR fills come after the core layers, so
  they draw over the diagram slots; if that washes out a diagram on device, move the slots after the Israel fills for that
  region in `registry.ts` (Swift raised them for the same reason).
- The PDF viewer reports an unopenable file through `onError`, which turns the loaded state into the failed state.
- `FitToViewPDFView` min/max scale (fit to 8x fit) maps to `minScale 1`, `maxScale 8` of react-native-pdf; on-device check pending.

## Not done / needs a device

- `AirportChartsSection` (the chart list in the airport sheet) depends on the terminal procedure service (S1c) and the
  airport sheet; it follows with I1.
- The diagram overlay and PDF viewer are not exercised on a device; the `ImageSource` keyed remount for a reused slot, the
  corner order and react-native-pdf scaling need the CI capture or an emulator run. A fidelity capture state for a zoomed-in
  airport with a diagram needs a fixture pack and a Swift reference image (see M1.md, Fidelity).
