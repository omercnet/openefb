# U8 port notes: recording, replay, debrief and flight detail screens

U8 is the second half of the app-screens track. U8a (the recording controls) is first; U8b (replay, debrief, flight detail)
adds its section below it. The screens sit on the L4 recording coordinator (U8a) and the L8 replay and L5 debrief engines (U8b).
No navigation and no app-startup wiring here; that is the app shell (I1).

## U8a: recording controls

### Ported

- `ViewModels/RecordingViewModel.swift` -> `src/ui/state/recordingStore.ts`, a vanilla Zustand store over `RecordingCoordinator`:
  `startRecording`, `press` (what a tap on the record button does), `requestStop` / `cancelStop` / `confirmStop`
  (stop, store the plan legs, persist through `FlightPersistenceService`, map `savedTrackUnreadable` and `saveFailed` to the
  `recordingFailed` errors), `cancelCountdown`, `dismissError`, `dismissDiscardedNotice`, the microphone flag.
  The pure rules are in `src/domain/recording/recordingNotices.ts`: `problemSeverity`, `shouldShowDiscardedNotice`, `pressAction`,
  the notice copy (verbatim from Swift) and `problemDetail` (the cause without the "Recording failed:" prefix).
- `Views/Map/RecordingOverlayView.swift`: `RecordingNotices` -> `src/ui/recording/RecordingNotices.tsx` (microphone off, a
  recording problem with its severity, recording not saved; the 4 pt leading rule in the notice colour; 60 pt buttons) and the
  "End recording?" confirmation -> `StopRecordingDialog.tsx`. The glyphs the notices need are in `ui/recording/icons.tsx`.
- `RecordButton`, `AudioLevelMeter`, `formattedElapsedTime` and the lit segments are U3's (`ui/flightLog/RecordButton.tsx`, #47).
  This ticket does not duplicate them: the record button binds to `store.getState().press()` and reads `status`,
  `elapsedTime`, `audioLevel` and `countdownRemaining` from the store.
- New seam `MicrophonePermission` (`domain/ports/microphonePermission.ts`, fake included) with an expo-audio adapter
  (`adapters/microphonePermission.ts`): Swift read `AVAudioApplication.shared.recordPermission` directly, which has no JS
  equivalent. `openSettings` is `Linking.openSettings()` (Swift: `UIApplication.openSettingsURLString`).

### Seams the app shell (I1) fills

- Create the store with `createRecordingStore(deps)`. `deps.coordinator` is the `RecordingCoordinator`, `deps.microphone` is
  `createMicrophonePermission()`, `deps.persistence` is the `FlightPersistenceService`. `deps.activeProfileIds` and
  `deps.activeRouteLegs` read what Swift read from `AppState` (`activeAircraftProfileID`, `activePilotProfileID`,
  `activeRoute.legs`). `deps.saveRouteLegs` is `RecordingDatabase.saveRouteLegs`.
- Mount `<RecordingNotices store />` in the strip under the record button and `<StopRecordingDialog store />` once.
- The record button calls `press()`. It returns `'problem'` when a start went ahead with the microphone off or failed: that is
  where the shell gives the warning haptic.
- Other screens that read `AppState.recordingStatus`, `currentFlightPhase`, `recordingElapsedTime`, `audioLevel` or
  `activeFlightRecordID` (the log strip, Flight Detail disabling Replay) read the store instead of a copy.
- Nothing else drives the store: it follows the coordinator, so an auto-started recording needs no call.

### Not ported

- `recentTranscripts` (last five live transcripts). The L4 `RecordingState` has no such field, and no Swift view reads
  `AppState.recentTranscripts` (the shell appends to it; nothing displays it).
- Haptics on the record button (shell), the `AppState` mirror writes (see the seam above), the 0.5 s sync loop (see below).

### Swift differences

- **No polling.** Swift copied the coordinator's state every 0.5 s. The store subscribes to the coordinator, so a countdown tick,
  a failure or the end of a recording shows at once. The coordinator's `lastError` is still surfaced once and cleared.
- **Stop confirmation.** Swift's `confirmationDialog` is a popover anchored on the button (iPad) or an action sheet (iPhone).
  The port is a centred modal with the same title, message and two choices, on both platforms.
- **Microphone permission is asked through the port, on a start only.** `undetermined` is asked once (the system prompt) as in
  Swift. A `denied` answer starts the recording anyway, with the notice (the GPS track still records), as in Swift.
  Android: `canAskAgain` does not matter, a refusal is `denied`; a response that says `granted: false` while its status says
  granted counts as `undetermined`.
- **The microphone notice is re-read when the app returns to the foreground** (`refreshMicrophoneOnForeground`, mounted by
  `RecordingNotices`). Swift read it on every 0.5 s tick, which is how a permission given in Settings made the notice go. The
  notice shows only while a recording runs, as in Swift.
- **The notice buttons** are the platform's: the "Dismiss" and "Open Settings" pills are `infoFill` with the accent label (the
  SwiftUI `.bordered` large style).

### JS-only guards (not in Swift; each has a test)

- A second start or stop while one runs is refused (`busy`), so a double tap cannot start two flights or persist one twice.
- A stop that throws (the coordinator's promise can reject; Swift's cannot) is shown as a `recordingFailed` problem, and the
  store is not left busy.
- A `persist` that rejects is a `saveFailed` result with the same notice as Swift gives for it.
- The stop dialog closes by itself if the recording ends some other way (a stale dialog would stop nothing).
- A microphone answer still on its way when the recording state changes is dropped (it belongs to the earlier recording).
- A permission check that fails is shown as an audio problem (`audioSessionFailed`); the recording still starts.
- A plan that cannot be stored with the flight (`saveRouteLegs` rejects) is ignored, like Swift's `try?`; the recording and
  the flight are not affected.
- A Settings page that cannot be opened is ignored (Swift `openURL` does not report it either).

### Tests

Ported by name: `RecordingProblemSeverityTests` (2: the caution/warning split) and `MapGPSRecordingFixesTests`'
`discardedEmptyShowsNoticeSavedDoesNot` (`recordingNotices.test.ts`, 20 tests with the copy and press rules).
The rest of `MapGPSRecordingFixesTests` is other tickets': the `GPSFailureReason` cases (A1), Clear Direct-To (map) and the
debrief eligibility cases (U8b); `RecordingStopPathCharacterizationTests`' `TrackRecorder` suite is A1's. Swift has no other
test of the view model, so the store's 46 tests are written from the Swift source, over a real `RecordingCoordinator` with the
L4 fakes: following the coordinator, start, press, the stop dialog, stop and persistence, the problems, the microphone, Settings.
`microphonePermission` (port 4, adapter 9) and `refreshMicrophoneOnForeground` (4) complete the 83 tests of U8a.
Every guard was mutation-checked (a mutant that removes it fails a test).

### Fidelity capture (Q1)

Three scenes are registered in `ios-capture.sh`, `android-capture.sh` and the launch list in `app/index.tsx`, no floors:
`recording` (microphone off and the audio failing: both amber), `recordingstopped` (after a stop with no track: the failure in
red and the not-saved notice) and `recordingstop` (the confirmation). They run a real store over a coordinator on fakes with a
clock that stands still. The web preview adds buttons that drive the store (press the record button, no track on the next
stop, audio fails, microphone off or on); they are web only and do not appear in a native capture. As for U7, there is no
SwiftUI reference to compare against (the Swift debug hooks open only `map` and `log`).
