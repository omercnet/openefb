# L8 port notes: replay engine

Source: `efb-212/Services/Replay/ReplayEngine.swift` -> `src/domain/replay/replayEngine.ts` (+ `types.ts`).
Tests: 20 of 20 `ReplayEngineTests` cases ported (same names and data; the four tick-speed cases are one `test.each`),
plus 2 added tests for the timer, end-of-flight pause, audio sync, mute and drift correction (unreachable in Swift, which
had no audio file in tests).

## Differences

- Data comes from an injected `ReplayStore` (async) instead of `RecordingDatabase`; `loadFlight` is async.
- Audio is an injected `ReplayAudioPlayer`. Swift's `FileManager.fileExists` check, `AVAudioPlayer` creation and
  `enableRate = true` belong to the adapter, which passes null when there is no audio file.
- The 20 Hz `Timer` is an injected `TickScheduler`; `intervalScheduler` (setInterval) is the default.
- Coordinates are `{ lat, lon }`, times epoch ms (audio segment times stay seconds), `playbackSpeed` is a number.
- `@Observable` is replaced by `subscribe()`. `TrackPoint` and friends are structural subsets in `types.ts` until D lands.

## Swift behaviour preserved

- Heading is interpolated linearly, so 350 -> 10 degrees swings through 180 instead of wrapping (Swift bug, kept).
- `testTick` bypasses the isPlaying guard and does not pause at the end, as in Swift.
- `cleanup()` does not reset position or data.
- `setSpeed` from 4x/8x down to 1x/2x while playing does not start the audio player (it was never started while muted);
  drift correction only corrects a player that is already playing, so audio stays silent until pause and play. Silence also persists when playback starts at 4x or 8x
  and later drops to 1x/2x; if playback starts at 1x/2x, `setSpeed(4)` only mutes the running player and 1x restores it.
  Swift bug, kept per the porting method.
- `loadFlight` does not stop or release a previously attached audio player when called again; a later call without audio
  keeps the old player. Swift bug, kept; callers use a fresh engine or `cleanup()` between flights.
- `loadFlight` reads all three collections before assigning and ignores a superseded call (JS-only guard for async stores).
