# Pilot interview kit (efb-77r.7)

Goal: replace the assumptions in docs/CUSTOMER.md with evidence. Five 20-minute conversations with
weekend VFR pilots. Method: The Mom Test, which means asking about their last real flights, never
pitching OpenEFB, and letting them talk 80% of the time.

## Who to recruit
- Private or Sport certificate, VFR, flew in the last 30 days.
- Uses an iPad (any EFB) in the cockpit.
- Mix: 2 owners, 2 club/rental pilots, 1 student pilot. At least 1 who flies at night.
- Not friends of the project: use flying-club boards, r/flying, a local FBO.

Recruiting post:
> Looking for 5 VFR pilots for a 20-minute chat about how you use your iPad in the cockpit. No sales
> pitch, I just want to hear about your last few flights. I'll buy you a coffee (or $20 gift card).

## Opening (1 min)
"I'm trying to understand how pilots actually use iPads when flying VFR. There are no right answers, and
I'm not selling anything. Can you walk me through your last flight?"

## Questions (past behaviour only)
**Anchor job, J1 in-flight awareness**
1. Walk me through your last flight from the moment you got in the plane. When did you look at the iPad? What were you looking for?
2. Was there a moment you looked at it and something didn't look right or you weren't sure? What happened next?
3. When did you last lose GPS, signal, or battery in the air? What did you do?
4. Before your last flight, what did you download or check so it would work in the air? How did you know it was ready?
5. Last time you flew at night: how did you set up the screen?

**Trust (assumed worst dimension)**
6. Tell me about a time an app showed you something that turned out to be wrong or old. How did you find out?
7. How do you know the chart or weather you're looking at is current?

**Emergency (J3)**
8. Have you ever had to divert, or seriously thought about it? What did you look at to pick where to go?

**Alternatives and habit**
9. What else is in the cockpit that tells you where you are (panel GPS, phone, paper)? Last flight, which one did you trust most, and why?
10. What did you use before your current EFB? Why did you switch? What would make you switch again? (Answer from what happened, not hypotheticals.)
11. What do you pay for EFB software today? Who pays?

**Learn (J4)**
12. After your last flight, what did you do? Logbook, debrief, review anything?

## Deflect, don't collect
- Compliments ("that sounds great") → "Thanks. Last time that came up, what did you do?"
- Feature ideas → "What happened the last time you needed that?"
- Generics ("I always…") → "Tell me about the most recent time."

## Close (the ask)
"Can I send you a TestFlight build to try on one real flight, then do a 10-minute call after?"
Record yes/no. Yes plus an actual flight is the commitment we're looking for.
